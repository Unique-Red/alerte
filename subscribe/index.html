Frequently Asked Questions

With Family Circle, one family manager pays a single consolidated subscription (₦6,000 or £30/month) that covers up to 5 total family members. Each invited member receives full Premium protection, including 24/7 one-swipe SOS, group emergency broadcasts, unlimited 5km radar, and live circle location check-ins.
After subscribing, you will receive your unique Family Circle code (e.g. https://alerteuniversal.com/join-family?code=XYZ892). You can share this link with up to 4 other family members to join your protective circle instantly.
When any member swipes the Emergency SOS, high-priority audible alerts and live GPS tracking are immediately sent to all other members of your circle. Simultaneously, the incident is routed to local emergency authorities, police, and rapid-response security agencies.
For GBP (£) payments, we use Stripe (supporting credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay). For NGN (₦) payments, we use Paystack (supporting debit cards, bank transfer, and USSD).
